UNIT 1 • LETTERS, NUMBERS AND ME

Writing Practice

Now it's your turn! Complete these two writing activities in your notebook.

WRITING ACTIVITY
01

Write Your Full Name

Write your first name and family name in your notebook. Remember to use a capital letter at the beginning of each name.

EXAMPLE

Sophia Marie Johnson

Each name begins with a capital letter.

NUMBERS 1–10
02

Write Numbers as Words

Write the numbers from 1 to 10 in English. Use words instead of digits, and check your spelling carefully.

EXAMPLE

1 — one   2 — two   3 — three

Continue writing the remaining numbers.
