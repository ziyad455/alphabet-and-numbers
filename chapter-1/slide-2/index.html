Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

  1. 01

    I can recognize and identify uppercase and lowercase English letters.

  2. 02

    I can say the letters of the English alphabet in order with understandable pronunciation.

  3. 03

    I can recognize, pronounce, and tell apart numbers 1–20, especially the pronunciation of 13–19.

  4. 04

    I can answer at least 4 out of 5 basic personal questions using simple words or short phrases.

  5. 05

    I can spell my first and family names aloud using English letter names.

  6. 06

    I can write my full name using appropriate capitalization.

  7. 07

    I can correctly identify at least 4 out of 5 dictated letters and 4 out of 5 dictated numbers.
