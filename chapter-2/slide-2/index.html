Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, I can:

  1. 01

    Greetings

    Use common greetings, introductions, and farewells in short everyday conversations.

  2. 02

    Personal Information

    Ask and answer simple questions about names, countries, nationalities, ages, and where people live.

  3. 03

    The Verb “To Be”

    Use subject pronouns with am, is, and are to make simple sentences about myself and others.

  4. 04

    Listening

    Recognize familiar expressions and personal information in short introductory conversations.

  5. 05

    Numbers 20–50

    Recognize and say numbers from 20 to 50.
